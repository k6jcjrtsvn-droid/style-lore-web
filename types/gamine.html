Kibbe body type

Gamine

Compact, mixed, playful.

One outfit in Gamine lines for each wardrobe — a starting idea, not a rule.

Your frame is small and neat, and your features blend both angular and curved qualities rather than settling on one. Clothes that mix textures or proportions in unexpected combinations — a structured jacket with a soft skirt — read better on you than anything too uniform or too matronly.

What works on a Gamine

  • Compact, mixed shapes: a fitted top with a straight skirt, a short jacket over a slim dress.
  • Crisp fabrics and lively details — contrast trim, buttons, small graphic prints.
  • Broken lines and a bit of contrast in every outfit; a mix of sharp and soft.
  • Small-to-medium, playful accessories: a beret, a small crossbody, a stud earring.

What fights your lines

  • Long, heavy, flowing pieces that overwhelm a small frame.
  • Very plain, monochrome, unbroken looks that read flat on you.
  • Large-scale accessories and big prints.

Three outfits to start with

A fitted striped top, a cropped cardigan and a straight mini skirt.
A short trench over a slim knit dress with flat ankle boots.
A collared blouse with contrast buttons, tapered trousers and loafers.

Most often confused with

Soft Gamine — adds roundness and wants softer fabrics; pure Gamine is crisper and more mixed.
