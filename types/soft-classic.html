Kibbe body type

Soft Classic

Balanced lines, softened.

You carry Classic's even, balanced proportions with an added gentleness in the flesh and features. Tailored pieces still look best, but with softer fabrics, rounded necklines, and slightly more curve in the silhouette than a pure Classic would choose.

What works on a Soft Classic

  • Balanced shapes softened at the edges: a rounded lapel, a slightly gathered sleeve, a soft sheath.
  • Smooth fabrics with a little drape — crepe de chine, soft wool, matte jersey.
  • Gentle waist definition and rounded necklines.
  • Refined, slightly delicate accessories: a small pendant, a rounded-toe pump, a soft leather bag.

What fights your lines

  • Sharp, angular tailoring and hard geometric details.
  • Anything heavy, chunky or oversized.
  • Overly ornate or frilly pieces — soft, not fussy.

Three outfits to start with

A blush wrap blouse, straight cream trousers and rounded-toe flats.
A soft sheath dress with a slim belt and a small structured bag.
A fine cardigan over a scoop-neck top with a flowing midi skirt.

Most often confused with

Soft Natural — both are soft, but Soft Natural is broader and more relaxed; Soft Classic stays polished and symmetrical.
