Kibbe body type

Natural

Blunt, broad, unfussy.

One outfit in Natural lines for each wardrobe — a starting idea, not a rule.

Your bone structure and features read broad and blunt rather than sharp or curved — a genuinely relaxed, athletic line. The most flattering clothes are similarly unstructured: soft tailoring, natural fibers, easy movement, and nothing that fights the body with tight seaming or delicate detail.

What works on a Natural

  • Clean, easy shapes with a relaxed fit: a straight coat, a boxy jacket, a simple shift.
  • Matte, textured fabrics — cotton, wool, linen, suede — in earthy or muted tones.
  • Minimal ornamentation; let the texture and the cut do the talking.
  • Practical, mid-scale accessories: a leather satchel, a plain belt, a wooden bangle.

What fights your lines

  • Stiff, sharp tailoring with exaggerated shoulders.
  • Ornate detail — ruffles, sequins, lace — reads fussy against blunt, broad lines.
  • Very fitted, body-conscious silhouettes that fight the width of the frame.

Three outfits to start with

A boxy denim jacket, a plain tee, wide cropped trousers and white sneakers.
A relaxed wool coat over a rib knit dress with ankle boots.
A linen shirt half-tucked into tapered chinos, leather sandals.

Most often confused with

Soft Natural — the softer sibling adds curve and wants gentle waist definition; pure Natural keeps it straighter.
