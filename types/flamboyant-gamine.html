Flamboyant Gamine

Compact frame, sharp contrast.

One outfit in Flamboyant Gamine lines for each wardrobe — a starting idea, not a rule.

You're compact in scale with features or coloring that create real contrast — dark against light, sharp against round. Play that contrast up rather than smoothing it over: mix unexpected pieces, bold color blocking, and playful proportions in petite scale.

What works on a Flamboyant Gamine

  • Sharp, compact tailoring: a cropped jacket, a boxy blazer, a straight mini skirt.
  • Crisp fabrics with graphic contrast — bold stripes, colorblocking, geometric prints.
  • Broken-up lines and mixed pieces rather than one long silhouette.
  • Playful, angular accessories: a chunky sneaker, a geometric earring, a small structured bag.

What fights your lines

  • Long, unbroken flowing lines that swallow a compact frame.
  • Soft, draped, romantic detail.
  • Oversized pieces with no shape.

Three outfits to start with

A cropped leather jacket, striped tee, straight jeans and loafers.
A boxy tweed jacket with a mini skirt and ankle boots.
A colorblocked knit with tapered trousers and a chunky sneaker.

Most often confused with

Gamine — pure Gamine is more mixed and playful; Flamboyant Gamine leans sharper and a touch longer in line.
