Support

Last updated 23 September 2026

Email support@style-lore.com and a real person will read it. We aim to reply within two business days. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — and which you are using, the website or the Android or iOS app.

Your quiz and your result

The Kibbe quiz takes about two minutes and needs no account. If your result does not feel like you, retake it — the questions are about bone structure and proportion, not about how you like to dress, and people often answer the first one or two from habit. Your style words come from a separate set of questions and are meant to sit alongside your type, not override it.

Photos and privacy

The photo analysis runs on your device. Photos you point the camera at for a Kibbe or garment read are not uploaded, not stored on our servers, and never seen by us. Photos you deliberately attach to a Community post or a closet item are uploaded, because that is what posting means. The Privacy Policy spells out exactly what is stored and where.

Your closet

Closet items sync to your account when you are signed in, so they follow you between the website and the apps. Marking an item Public puts it in the community Closets tab; Private keeps it to you. If pieces look duplicated after signing in on a second device, email us — do not delete them one by one, we can usually sort it out from our side.

Premium, billing and refunds

Premium is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with the first week free, and it renews until you cancel.

  • Bought on the website: cancel from Home → Manage subscription.
  • Bought in the iPhone app: cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings. We cannot cancel or refund an Apple purchase for you — Apple handles both.
  • Bought in the Android app: cancel in Google Play → Subscriptions, and refunds follow Google's policy.

Cancelling stops the next charge; you keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for. If Premium did not do what we said it would, email us within 14 days of the charge and we will refund a website purchase in full.

Reporting and blocking someone

Every Community post and every comment has a Report option. Reports are reviewed, and content reported by enough different people is hidden automatically while we look.

You can also block anyone from their profile. Blocking hides their posts, comments and stories from you and yours from them, stops messages in both directions, and removes any follows between you. They are not told. Undo it any time under Home → Blocked accounts.

Deleting your account

Home → Delete my account, in the app or on the website. It removes your account, profile and Community posts and clears your likes and follows. It cannot be undone, and it does not by itself cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google — cancel that in the store first.

Something is broken

Email support@style-lore.com with a screenshot if you can. Errors are logged on our side too, so if you tell us roughly when it happened we can usually find it.
