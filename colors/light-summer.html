Color season

Light Summer

Cool and light, with soft, powdery coloring.

The Light Summer palette

Six colors that carry this season. They are a direction, not a uniform — the point is the family they belong to, not these exact swatches.

What defines it

UndertoneCool
DepthLight
ClarityMuted
ContrastLow

Keep the values close. A low-contrast outfit — one family of tones head to toe — reads as intentional on you, while a hard light-against-dark split looks like the clothes are wearing you.

Colors that work

Cool and powdery — soft blue, lilac, rose, sage. Keep the contrast low.

Colors that fight it

Black, hot brights, and orange-based warms.

Neutrals to build on

Cool soft neutrals: soft navy, greyed taupe, oyster, cocoa with a rose cast, and a grey that leans blue rather than yellow. Soft white beats stark white.

Metals

Silver, platinum and white gold. Yellow gold usually reads slightly dirty against cool skin.

Most often confused with Light Spring

Same lightness, opposite undertone. If gold jewellery looks slightly dirty on you and silver looks clean, you are in Light Summer.
