Color season

True Summer

Cool and muted, with medium depth and a gentle contrast.

The True Summer palette

Six colors that carry this season. They are a direction, not a uniform — the point is the family they belong to, not these exact swatches.

What defines it

UndertoneCool
DepthMedium
ClarityMuted
ContrastMedium

Moderate contrast. You can put a lighter piece against a darker one, but the jump should be a step, not a cliff.

Colors that work

Cool and gently greyed — rose, teal, soft navy, plum.

Colors that fight it

Orange, warm golds, neon, and stark black-against-white.

Neutrals to build on

Cool soft neutrals: soft navy, greyed taupe, oyster, cocoa with a rose cast, and a grey that leans blue rather than yellow. Soft white beats stark white.

Metals

Silver, platinum and white gold. Yellow gold usually reads slightly dirty against cool skin.

Most often confused with Soft Summer

Both cool and both muted. True Summer can still carry a color that reads clearly — a real rose, a real teal. Soft Summer needs everything greyed a step further before it settles.
