Color season

True Winter

Cool and clear, with striking, high-contrast coloring.

The True Winter palette

Six colors that carry this season. They are a direction, not a uniform — the point is the family they belong to, not these exact swatches.

What defines it

UndertoneCool
DepthMedium
ClarityClear
ContrastHigh

Let the pieces contrast. A light piece against a dark one matches the contrast already in your own coloring; an all-mid-tone outfit can look like it has gone slightly grey.

Colors that work

Pure cool colors at full clarity — true red, royal blue, magenta, emerald, stark white and black.

Colors that fight it

Anything dusty, muted or warm-golden.

Neutrals to build on

Stark neutrals: true black, charcoal, pure white, deep navy. Winter is the one family that can wear real black next to real white and look better for it.

Metals

Silver, platinum and white gold. Yellow gold usually reads slightly dirty against cool skin.

Most often confused with Bright Winter

Both cool and clear. True Winter is balanced — it can wear stark black and white as the whole outfit. Bright Winter wants the intensity turned all the way up and looks slightly flat in plain black and white.
