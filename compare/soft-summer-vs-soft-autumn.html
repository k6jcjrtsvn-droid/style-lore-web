Soft Summer vs Soft Autumn

How to tell which one you actually are.

The short answer. The single most common mix-up in color analysis, because both are muted and neither can take a hard bright. Soft Summer is cool-muted and wants sage and mauve; Soft Autumn is warm-muted and wants camel and terracotta.

What separates them, from each side

Both guides describe this pair the same way from opposite directions, which is itself the useful part — the rule reads true whichever side you start from.

Read from Soft Summer

Just as soft, but warm. This is the most common mix-up in the system. A Soft Autumn comes alive in camel and terracotta and looks tired in mauve; a Soft Summer is the reverse.

Read from Soft Autumn

Just as soft, but cool. This is the most common mix-up in the system. A Soft Summer wants sage, mauve and dusty blue and looks tired in camel; a Soft Autumn comes alive in camel and looks tired in mauve.

Check it on yourself

The self-check from the Soft Summer guide

Do these in daylight, with a bare face if you can.

  • Hold camel and greyed mauve against your face. If mauve wins, you are on the cool side.
  • Hold soft white and cream. If soft white looks fresh and cream looks yellow, you are a summer.
  • Wear a clear rose pink as a whole top. If it looks slightly too much, stay here; if it looks like you, try True Summer.
The self-check from the Soft Autumn guide

Do these in daylight, with a bare face if you can.

  • Hold camel and greyed mauve against your face. If camel wins, you are on the warm side.
  • Hold cream and a soft, cool white. If cream looks fresh and the cool white looks flat, you are an autumn.
  • Wear rust as a whole top. If it looks slightly too much, stay here; if it looks like you, try True Autumn.
