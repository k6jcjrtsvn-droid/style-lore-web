Dramatic Classic vs Classic

How to tell which one you actually are.

The short answer. pure Classic is fully balanced; Dramatic Classic carries a little sharpness and can take a bolder shoulder or line.

Dramatic Classic

Classic

What separates them, from each side

Each guide describes the other from its own point of view, and the two tests are different. This is the only page that carries both.

Read from Dramatic Classic

Pure Classic is fully balanced and looks best with softer, rounder details than you do. A rounded lapel and a slightly curved hem look right on a Classic and a touch soft on you. If a sharp collar looks severe rather than polished, read the Classic guide.

Read from Classic

Dramatic Classic has your balance with a touch of sharpness. It wants a squarer shoulder and a crisper collar. If a sharp-lapel blazer looks better on you than a moderate one, and soft details look slightly limp, you may be a Dramatic Classic.

Check it on yourself

The self-check from the Dramatic Classic guide

Polished, overwhelm, and untidy: Dramatic Classic. Severe on the first points to Classic; proportionate on the second points to Dramatic.

  • Does a sharp, squared shoulder look polished on you, or severe?
  • Does a floor-length, single-breasted coat look proportionate, or does it overwhelm your frame?
  • Does a slouchy, unstructured jacket look relaxed, or untidy?
The self-check from the Classic guide

Best outfit, a touch hard, and right: Classic. Polished on the second points to Dramatic Classic; severe on the third points to Soft Classic.

  • Does a moderate, unremarkable blazer that fits perfectly look like your best outfit, or does it feel like it needs something?
  • Does a very sharp, squared shoulder look polished on you, or a touch hard?
  • Does a crisp, straight sheath look right on you, or a little severe, so that a softer draped version looks better?
