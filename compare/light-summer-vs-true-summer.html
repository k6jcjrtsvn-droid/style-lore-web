Light Summer vs True Summer

How to tell which one you actually are.

The short answer. Cool but deeper than you. A True Summer can wear a mid-depth plum, teal or soft navy as a whole piece and look finished; a Light Summer looks slightly weighed down by the same colors. If the mid-tones look like you and the pale ones look faint, look here.

What separates them, from each side

Each guide describes the other from its own point of view, and the two tests are different. This is the only page that carries both.

Read from Light Summer

Cool but deeper than you. A True Summer can wear a mid-depth plum, teal or soft navy as a whole piece and look finished; a Light Summer looks slightly weighed down by the same colors. If the mid-tones look like you and the pale ones look faint, look here.

Read from True Summer

Cool but lighter and more delicate than you. A Light Summer looks best in the palest version of every color and finds a mid-depth plum or teal slightly heavy. If your best colors are the pale ones, look here.

Check it on yourself

The self-check from the Light Summer guide

Do these in daylight, with a bare face if you can.

  • Hold peach and rose pink against your face. If rose wins, you are on the cool side.
  • Hold soft white and cream. If soft white looks fresh and cream looks yellow, you are a summer.
  • Wear a mid-depth plum as a whole top. If it looks heavy, stay here; if it looks like you, try True Summer.
The self-check from the True Summer guide

Do these in daylight, with a bare face if you can.

  • Wear a clear rose pink as a whole top. If it looks like you, you are a True Summer. If it looks too much, try Soft Summer; if it looks heavy, try Light Summer.
  • Hold soft white and cream against your jaw. If soft white looks fresh and cream looks yellow, you are a summer.
  • Hold soft navy and black. If navy looks calm and black looks harsh, stay in the summer family.
