Deep Autumn vs Deep Winter

How to tell which one you actually are.

The short answer. Both go dark and both take contrast. Deep Autumn&#x27;s darks have brown and bronze in them; Deep Winter&#x27;s have blue and black. Compare chocolate against true black — one of them will look like it belongs on you.

What separates them, from each side

Each guide describes the other from its own point of view, and the two tests are different. This is the only page that carries both.

Read from Deep Autumn

Just as dark, but cool. A Deep Winter looks alive in true black and pure white and drained by camel; a Deep Autumn is the reverse. Compare chocolate with black against your face, and cream with pure white.

Read from Deep Winter

Just as dark, but warm. A Deep Autumn's darks have brown and bronze inside them and a Deep Autumn looks alive in chocolate and camel; a Deep Winter looks drained by both. If black looks like it belongs on you and chocolate looks like a compromise, you are on the winter side.

Check it on yourself

The self-check from the Deep Autumn guide

Do these in daylight, with a bare face if you can.

  • Hold chocolate and black against your face. If chocolate is clearly better, you are an autumn.
  • Hold cream and pure white. If cream is clean and white is harsh, you are an autumn.
  • Wear rust as a whole top. If it looks as good as burgundy does, you may be a True Autumn; if burgundy wins, stay here.
The self-check from the Deep Winter guide

Do these in daylight, with a bare face if you can.

  • Hold black and dark chocolate against your face. If black is clearly better, you are a winter.
  • Hold pure white and cream. If white is clean and cream is dingy, you are a winter.
  • Wear an icy pastel as a whole top. If it looks crisp, you may be a True Winter; if it looks thin, stay here.
