Gamine vs Soft Gamine

How to tell which one you actually are.

The short answer. adds roundness and wants softer fabrics; pure Gamine is crisper and more mixed.

Gamine

Soft Gamine

What separates them, from each side

Each guide describes the other from its own point of view, and the two tests are different. This is the only page that carries both.

Read from Gamine

Soft Gamine is the same compact scale with more roundness in the flesh. It wants a fit-and-flare where you want a straight skirt, and a softer fabric where you want a crisp one. If a straight knee-length skirt looks slightly boxy on you, you may be a Soft Gamine.

Read from Soft Gamine

Pure Gamine has the same compact scale with straighter flesh and a more even mix of sharp and round. It wears a straight skirt well; you wear a flared one better. If a straight, boxy jacket looks fine on you, you may be closer to Gamine.

Check it on yourself

The self-check from the Gamine guide

Lively, neat, and costume: you are a Gamine. Out of place on the first question points to Flamboyant Gamine; square on the second points to Soft Gamine.

  • Does a small, rounded detail (a Peter Pan collar, a small bow) look lively on you, or does it look out of place?
  • Does a straight knee-length skirt look neat and finished, or slightly square?
  • When you dress all in one mood, entirely crisp or entirely soft, does it look like you or like a costume?
The self-check from the Soft Gamine guide

Flared, right, and natural: Soft Gamine. Straight on the first points to Gamine; wanting something softer on the second points to Theatrical Romantic.

  • Does a short flared skirt look better on you than a straight one of the same length?
  • Does a small, crisp collar look right, or does it look too plain and you find yourself wanting something softer?
  • Does a puff sleeve or a rounded shoulder look natural on you, or oversized?
